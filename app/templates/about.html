IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

What the model estimates

A transparent account of the current project choices and their limits.

TARGET

Forward drawdown regression

For each observed session, the target is the maximum non-negative decline from that session’s close to the lowest low over the next user-specified number of observed sessions. The final rows without a full future window remain unlabeled.

A threshold flag is derived by comparing the regression estimate with the user’s threshold. It is not a calibrated probability.

FEATURE SELECTION

RFE plus Boruta

The supplied project notebook uses recursive feature elimination with XGBoost and Boruta with a random forest. The project selects the intersection, falling back to the union when the intersection is empty. Selection is fit on training rows only.

VALIDATION

Chronological holdout

Rows remain chronological. A horizon-sized gap is purged at split boundaries. The validation partition controls early stopping; the test partition is reserved for final evaluation. DNN scaling is fit on training rows only.

RESEARCH LIMITATION

Not an exact paper reproduction

The full paper was not available to inspect. The accessible abstract describes regression and MSE/MAE/RMSE, but does not establish exact per-stock crisis labels, dataset constituents, HFS configuration, or model parameters. Current windows and settings are project implementation choices based on the supplied notebook, not verified paper defaults.
